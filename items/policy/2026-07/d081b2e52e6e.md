Export of trusted US AI and digital technology to partner countries. Proposed rules create a federal program helping foreign governments buy US cyber, cloud, AI chips and models instead of cheaper Chinese alternatives, with misuse assessments, end-use monitoring and $500 million in funding. National security and human rights are involved.
