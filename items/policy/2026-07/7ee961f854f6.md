AI literacy practices under EU AI rules. A searchable repository of more than 40 AI literacy initiatives, from e-learning to bootcamps, supports providers and deployers meeting the AI literacy requirement in force since February 2025, without conferring automatic compliance. Organizations and staff using AI are involved.
