Transparency and labeling of AI-generated content in the European Union. A voluntary code of practice offers a way to meet AI regulation transparency duties applying from August 2026, requiring generative AI providers to mark synthetic outputs machine-readably and deployers to disclose deepfakes. AI providers and detection vendors are involved.
