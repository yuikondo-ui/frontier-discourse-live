Testing advanced AI for nuclear incident and loss-of-control risks. A proposed US measure would create a seven-year national laboratory program to red-team frontier AI models for nuclear risks, requiring large developers to provide model access including unmitigated versions, with penalties up to $1 million per day. National security is involved.
