AI literacy obligations under EU AI regulation. Guidance explains that providers and deployers of AI systems must support AI literacy among staff, with amendments removing a mandated level, advice to tailor training to roles and risks, and staff training for human oversight of high-risk systems. Organizations and workers are involved.
