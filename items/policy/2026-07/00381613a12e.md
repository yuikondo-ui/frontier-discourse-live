Children's online safety rules covering chatbots, social platforms and gaming. A proposed federal package includes chatbot restrictions, disclosure duties, a study of chatbots and minors' mental health, age verification for sexual content, and children's privacy measures. Children, teens, parents and online platforms are affected.
