Tax treatment and environmental disclosure for AI data centers in the United States. Proposed rules would exclude AI data center property from bonus depreciation unless facilities meet high green-building standards, and require large operators to report water withdrawal, electricity consumption and peak demand. Utilities and local communities are affected.
