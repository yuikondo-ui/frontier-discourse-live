AI literacy, skills and talent policy in the EU. AI literacy duties for providers and deployers are amended so no specific level is mandated, with national enforcement from August 2026, alongside an AI skills academy, talent attraction schemes and upskilling for workers facing displacement. Workers, small firms and member states are involved.
