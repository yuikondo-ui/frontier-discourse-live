AI-enabled fraud targeting older adults. Lawmakers describe deepfakes and voice cloning making scams more effective, propose a scam-reporting portal, law enforcement training and a national anti-scam strategy, and question companion-app companies about safeguards. Victims of impersonation and cloned-voice kidnapping scams are involved.
