EU strategy for AI research, development and deployment. The EU invests over EUR 1 billion annually in AI research, aims to mobilize EUR 20 billion per year, funds AI factories and launches a frontier AI challenge for sovereign European models of at least 400 billion parameters. European researchers and industry are involved.
