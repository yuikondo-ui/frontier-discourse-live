Transparency obligations under EU AI rules. Guidance explains that AI systems interacting with people must disclose they are AI, generative outputs must carry machine-readable marking, and deepfakes and AI-generated public-interest text must be labeled, with some exemptions. AI providers and deployers in the EU are affected.
