AI-powered screening centres for cancer and cardiovascular disease across the EU. A network of centres pilots approved AI tools for prevention, early detection and diagnosis, generating real-world evidence on safety and cost-efficiency, aiming to cover all member states by 2029. Patients and underserved areas are involved.
