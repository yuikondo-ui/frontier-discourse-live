State oversight of frontier AI models through independent verification. A California executive order accelerates laws creating certified AI auditors, and directs experts to recommend onsite verifiers at AI labs, safety framework checks, an emergency kill switch for frontier models, and loss-of-control incident reporting. AI labs and state agencies are involved.
