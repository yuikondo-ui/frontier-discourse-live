Enforcement of EU AI regulation by market surveillance authorities. National authorities and an EU AI office enforce prohibitions, high-risk rules and general-purpose model duties, with powers to access documentation and source code, require corrections, impose penalties and accept complaints. AI providers and EU member states are affected.
