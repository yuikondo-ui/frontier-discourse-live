Children's online safety rules for companion chatbots and social media in California. A package of state laws regulates companion chatbots, bans addictive feed features for minors, restricts targeted advertising, protects student data and pauses AI toys, with lawmakers and advocates praising it. Children, schools and technology companies are affected.
