Algorithmic rent pricing and antitrust enforcement. A proposed settlement bars a landlord from revenue management software using competitors' sensitive data, amid allegations that a software firm pools nonpublic lease data into AI-branded pricing algorithms that align rents and entrench market dominance. Renters and housing competition are affected.
