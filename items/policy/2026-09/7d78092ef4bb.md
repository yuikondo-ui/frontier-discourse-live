AI for defending critical infrastructure from cyberattacks. A proposed US five-year pilot program would let critical infrastructure operators use department-procured AI and technical help to detect, test for and fix cyber vulnerabilities, prioritizing nonprofit, public, rural and small operators. Utilities and infrastructure owners are involved.
