Children's online safety and chatbot regulation in California. New state laws require companion chatbots to have crisis protocols for suicidal ideation, parental controls and independent child safety audits. Others ban addictive feeds for under-16s, cover AI-generated sexual imagery of minors, and regulate student data and chatbot-enabled toys.
