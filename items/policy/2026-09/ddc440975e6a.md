New state laws on AI at work, in healthcare, in transparency and against deepfakes: human review of automated discipline and firing, disclosure of AI-caused layoffs, limits on workplace surveillance, doctors' final judgment, watermark protection, likeness rights and gene synthesis screening. An order also defines AI and speeds independent oversight.
