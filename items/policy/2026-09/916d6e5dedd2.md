Accelerating AI adoption across European sectors. A summit in November 2026 marks one year of the EU strategy for applying AI, gathering industry, policymakers and startups to showcase trustworthy homegrown AI in healthcare, mobility, energy, public administration, manufacturing and defence. European businesses and governments are involved.
