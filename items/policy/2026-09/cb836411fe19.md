AI in education and workforce readiness in the United States. Proposed federal measures fund career pathways, micro-credentials, reskilling grants and tax credits, track occupations disrupted by AI, and set student and worker data privacy and human oversight rules for automated decisions. Schools, workers and AI developers are involved.
