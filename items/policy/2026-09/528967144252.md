Transparency requirements for widely used generative AI systems. Proposed US rules would require public disclosures from developers of models with large user bases on child safeguards, privacy, capabilities, safety practices, catastrophic risk evaluations, red-teaming and benchmarks. Minors, vulnerable users and consumer regulators are involved.
