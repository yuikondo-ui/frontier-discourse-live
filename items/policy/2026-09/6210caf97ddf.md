AI adoption across strategic economic sectors in Europe. A hybrid summit in Brussels marks one year of a European AI adoption strategy, gathering industry, policy and startup stakeholders to promote trustworthy, human-centric AI in healthcare, mobility, energy and public administration. The aim is European competitiveness built on homegrown AI.
