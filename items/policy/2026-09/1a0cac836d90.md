AI in healthcare for pediatric cancer research. Proposed US rules would appoint a federal AI innovation coordinator, codify a national childhood cancer data initiative and require AI-ready research data platforms supporting predictive modeling, biomarkers and trial design. Children with cancer and researchers are affected.
