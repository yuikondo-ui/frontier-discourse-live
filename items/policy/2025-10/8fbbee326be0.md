AI in healthcare for pediatric cancer research and treatment. A presidential directive tasks federal health bodies with improving data infrastructure, using AI to model patient response, disease progression and toxicity, derive biomarkers and improve clinical trial design, building on a $50 million annual childhood cancer data initiative.
