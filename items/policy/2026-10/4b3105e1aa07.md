Restrictions on outbound investment in sensitive technologies in countries of concern, covering AI systems, advanced semiconductors, quantum technology, high-performance computing and hypersonics. Proposed rules require notification of some transactions and prohibit others, expanding an existing program and country list. Investors and national security are affected.
