Vehicular terrorism and security risks of autonomous and connected vehicles. Proposed rules require a homeland security assessment of efforts to prevent vehicle-ramming attacks, including potential misuse of autonomous vehicles and cybersecurity threats to the AI-enabled software powering them. National security and public safety are involved.
