AI for scientific discovery through national laboratories in the United States. A federal initiative directs national laboratories to unite supercomputers and scientific data into an AI experimentation platform producing scientific foundation models and powering robotic labs. Biotechnology, energy, materials, quantum and semiconductor research are priority domains.
