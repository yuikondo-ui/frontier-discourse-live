AI regulation of general-purpose AI models in the European Union. Guidelines require technical documentation, copyright policies, training-data summaries and EU representatives, set compute thresholds for general-purpose and systemic-risk models, and impose evaluation, incident reporting and cybersecurity duties, with partial open-source exemptions and fines from August 2026.
