Security risks from connected and autonomous vehicles used in vehicle-ramming terrorism. A proposed federal requirement directs homeland security officials to assess threats including misuse of autonomous vehicles and cyberattacks on AI-enabled vehicle software, and to consider countermeasures such as geofencing and AI analytics. Law enforcement and industry are involved.
