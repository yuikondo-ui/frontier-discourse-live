AI regulation implementation in the European Union. A voluntary initiative offers webinars and workshops helping organisations prepare for binding EU AI rules, covering risk-based rules, provider obligations, general-purpose models, prohibited practices, AI literacy and support for small firms. Businesses and startups are involved.
