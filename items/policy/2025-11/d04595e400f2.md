Patent inventorship for AI-assisted inventions in the United States. Revised [PARTNER] guidance holds that AI systems cannot be inventors, treats generative AI as a tool like laboratory equipment, and applies the same human-conception standard whether or not AI was used. Inventors and patent applicants are affected.
