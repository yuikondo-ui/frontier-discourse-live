European initiatives to build an AI innovation ecosystem, including innovation hubs, testing facilities, supercomputing-based AI factories, regulatory sandboxes, a data and tools platform and an AI skills academy. Measures aim to ease compliance with AI regulation and speed adoption by businesses and workers.
