Algorithmic rent pricing software and antitrust law. A proposed settlement bars a landlord from using revenue management software built on competitors' sensitive data. Allegations hold that the software, partly marketed as AI, pools nonpublic landlord data to align rents and maintain a monopoly, harming renters.
