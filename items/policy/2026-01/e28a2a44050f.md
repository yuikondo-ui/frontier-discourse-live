Security of AI agents that plan and take actions autonomously on real-world systems. A US federal AI standards center requests input on risks such as indirect prompt injection, data poisoning and misaligned objectives, to inform voluntary guidelines and evaluations. Industry, academia, public safety and national security are involved.
