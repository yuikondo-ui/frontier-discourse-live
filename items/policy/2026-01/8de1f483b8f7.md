Security of AI agent systems. A US standards agency seeks public input on measuring and improving agent security, citing hijacking, backdoors, indirect prompt injection, data poisoning and misaligned objectives that could threaten public safety and critical infrastructure. Developers, deployers and critical infrastructure operators are involved.
