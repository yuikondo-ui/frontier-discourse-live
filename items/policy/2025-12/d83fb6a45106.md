Children's online safety and proposed US rules. Lawmakers weigh about 20 measures on child privacy, age verification, parental tools, addictive design and data limits, with disagreement over adequacy and enforcement capacity. Two measures would require informing people when they use AI chatbots and provide safe-use resources.
