AI for manufacturing productivity and critical infrastructure security. A US standards agency invests $20 million in two AI centers that develop AI agents and evaluations for manufacturing and for defending critical infrastructure from cyberthreats, aiming to sustain US AI leadership. Manufacturers and infrastructure operators are involved.
