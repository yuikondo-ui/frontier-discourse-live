Public-private collaboration on AI measurement science. A federal standards agency invites organizations to submit letters of interest to join its AI consortium developing scalable, interoperable AI metrics and techniques, requiring research agreements, with rolling reviews beginning June 2026. Industry, academia and government research partners are involved.
