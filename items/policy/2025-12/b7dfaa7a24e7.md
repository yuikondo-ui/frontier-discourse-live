AI in healthcare, specifically the adoption of AI tools in clinical care. A federal health department seeks public comment on accelerating clinical AI through risk-proportionate regulation, reimbursement changes and public-private research partnerships. Questions cover liability, privacy, evaluation methods and barriers facing private-sector developers.
