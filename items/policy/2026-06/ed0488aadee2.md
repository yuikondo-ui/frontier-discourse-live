Transparency and labelling of AI-generated content in the European Union. A voluntary code sets commitments for machine-readable marking and detection of generated audio, images, video and text, and for labelling deepfakes, supporting compliance with EU AI rules on marking and labelling. Generative AI providers and deployers are involved.
