Military AI and AI adoption across national security agencies. A presidential directive orders faster adoption of commercial and open-source models, high-security computing facilities, controllable systems with chain-of-command accountability, and updated autonomous weapons policy, and bars vendors from disabling AI systems without approval. Defense and intelligence agencies are involved.
