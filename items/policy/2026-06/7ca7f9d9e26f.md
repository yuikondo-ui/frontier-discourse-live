US federal policy on AI cybersecurity and frontier AI models. An executive order directs agencies to prioritize cyber defense of federal systems, expand AI-enabled defensive tools, create an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse with industry and develop a classified benchmark for AI cyber capabilities with voluntary pre-release access. National security agencies are involved.
