AI for pediatric cancer research and treatment. A US executive order directs a presidential health panel and science advisers to use AI to improve diagnosis, treatment and prevention of childhood cancer, expand a national childhood cancer data initiative and improve clinical trials. Patients and parents keep control of their data.
