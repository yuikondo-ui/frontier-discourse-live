AI in healthcare applied to pediatric cancer research. A federal directive instructs health agencies to accelerate AI-driven work on childhood cancer data, including AI-ready data infrastructure, trial participant selection, predictive modeling of treatment response and biomarker discovery. Children with cancer, researchers and clinicians are affected.
