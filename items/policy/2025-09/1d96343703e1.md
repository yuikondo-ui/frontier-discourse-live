Transparency obligations for AI systems in the European Union. A consultation on guidelines and a code of practice addresses rules requiring disclosure when people interact with AI, machine-readable marking of generative outputs, and notices for emotion recognition and deepfakes, citing risks of misinformation, fraud and impersonation.
