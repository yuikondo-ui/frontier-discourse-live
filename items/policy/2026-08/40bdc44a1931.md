Risk-based AI regulation in the European Union. The framework sets four risk tiers, banning practices such as harmful manipulation, social scoring and certain biometric uses, and imposing strict risk management, data quality, documentation and human oversight duties on high-risk uses in infrastructure, education, employment, law enforcement and justice.
