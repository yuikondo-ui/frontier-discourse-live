AI risks in the financial sector and financial stability oversight. Proposed rules would task a US financial stability council with coordinating regulators on AI threats such as market-manipulating deepfakes, liability gaps for AI agent transactions, infrastructure concentration and AI-enabled cyberattacks. Banks and financial regulators are involved.
