Taxing large foundation models to fund human jobs. Proposed federal rules impose an excise tax on developers and sellers of large models based on tokens processed or AI revenue, rising when unemployment exceeds 5%. Revenue funds grants for human jobs in care, education, infrastructure and journalism.
