Financial derivatives based on AI computing power. A US derivatives regulator seeks comment on listing contracts tied to the price of compute used by large language models, describing compute as a scarce, capital-intensive commodity and reviewing safeguards against manipulation. Financial markets and AI infrastructure investors are involved.
