The European Union's comprehensive AI regulation. A risk-based framework bans practices such as social scoring and manipulative techniques, imposes conformity assessment, oversight and incident reporting on high-risk systems in employment, education, credit and law enforcement, and sets transparency duties for chatbots and generated content.
