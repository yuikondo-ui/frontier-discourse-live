Harmonised technical standards for the EU AI Act. European standardisation bodies develop voluntary standards on risk management, data governance, transparency, human oversight, robustness and cybersecurity for high-risk AI systems, giving companies a presumption of conformity. Critical infrastructure and law enforcement uses are covered.
