Transparency obligations under EU AI rules. Guidelines explain that from August 2026 AI providers must disclose AI interactions and mark AI-generated content, while deployers must disclose emotion recognition, deepfakes and unreviewed AI-generated public-interest text, to counter misinformation and deception. National market surveillance authorities enforce the rules.
