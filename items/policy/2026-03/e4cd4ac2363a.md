Monitoring of AI systems after deployment in commercial and government settings. A federal standards body identifies six monitoring categories, including functionality, security, compliance and societal impacts, and lists gaps such as weak incident-sharing mechanisms, drawing on practitioner workshops and a literature review. Researchers, developers and public agencies are invited to comment.
