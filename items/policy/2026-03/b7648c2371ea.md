AI in healthcare policy and investment in Europe. EU plans claim AI in healthcare improves outcomes, sustainability and competitiveness, funding AI factories, health data infrastructures, cancer imaging and genomic platforms, under AI regulation alongside medical device rules. Patients, health systems and medical device makers are involved.
