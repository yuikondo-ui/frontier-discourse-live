Technical standards supporting AI regulation in the European Union. Harmonised standards translate legal requirements for high-risk AI systems into specifications covering risk management, data governance, logging, transparency, human oversight, robustness and cybersecurity, giving compliant providers a presumption of conformity, with first standards expected in 2026.
