Electricity costs from AI data centers and protection of household ratepayers. A presidential proclamation makes national policy a voluntary pledge by seven technology companies to keep data centers from raising household bills, by funding new generation, grid upgrades and separate rate structures. Utilities, states and local communities are involved.
