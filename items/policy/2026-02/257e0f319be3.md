Federal grants for small businesses developing AI and other emerging technologies. A standards agency awards $3.19 million to eight firms, including machine learning with hyperspectral imaging to monitor drug manufacturing cell cultures and an AI tool scoring manufacturers' cybersecurity practices. Other awards cover semiconductors, quantum and biotechnology.
