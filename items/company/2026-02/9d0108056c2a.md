AI agents performing many concurrent tasks in knowledge work. Research finds computer-using agents degrade sharply as concurrent interdependent tasks increase, and presents a framework using hierarchical planning, isolated subagents and tiered memory that improves completion rates. Memory rather than model capability may be the main bottleneck.
