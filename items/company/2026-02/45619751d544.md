Use of AI assistants in India's economy. Analysis of about one million conversations finds India ranks second globally in total use but 101st per capita, with use concentrated in software tasks and a few states, and users delegating more autonomy and gaining larger speedups. Broader access requires addressing income, infrastructure and awareness barriers.
