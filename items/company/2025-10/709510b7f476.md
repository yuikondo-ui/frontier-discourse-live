Automated auditing tools for AI safety behaviors. An open-source tool uses an auditing agent to probe AI models through simulated conversations, with LLM judges scoring deception, sycophancy, self-preservation and power-seeking across 14 frontier models and 111 scenarios. AI safety researchers are involved.
