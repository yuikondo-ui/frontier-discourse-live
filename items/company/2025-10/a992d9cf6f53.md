Long-running monitoring tasks for LLM agents. A research mechanism lets AI agents wait and monitor events by defining actions, completion conditions and adaptive polling intervals while saving state, raising reliability on one- to two-hour tasks from 5.6 percent to about 33 to 39 percent on a synthetic benchmark.
