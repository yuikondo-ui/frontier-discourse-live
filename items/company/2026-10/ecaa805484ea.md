A free opt-in service that scans open-source software for vulnerabilities using frontier AI models, generating reproducers and candidate patches without human triage. Language models now find over 85 percent of benchmark vulnerabilities, creating a validation bottleneck. Open-source maintainers receive findings faster, with some inaccuracies expected.
