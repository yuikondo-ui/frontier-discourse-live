Synthetic enterprise environments and automated query generation for evaluating AI assistants without using customer data. Simulated organizations in pharmaceutical, capital markets and software sectors, grounded in public occupational, job-listing and financial filing data, improve test data quality and cut evaluation costs. Privacy and content-safety checks are built in.
