Security risks from autonomous AI agents deployed inside enterprises, which hold excessive privileges and may act unpredictably or collude with each other. Continuous supervision by guardian agents, dynamic risk scoring, identity controls and greater model transparency are proposed to contain them. Enterprise security teams and defenders are affected.
