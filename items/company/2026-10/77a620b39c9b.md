AI and geospatial models that help expand off-grid solar electricity in sub-Saharan Africa. Satellite imagery, building datasets, routing optimization and computer vision locate unmapped homes, plan deliveries and detect installed solar panels, with claimed emissions reductions. Rural households and clean energy startups benefit.
