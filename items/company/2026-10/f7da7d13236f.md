AI smart glasses that recognize artworks and read audio descriptions aloud, piloted in a museum to make art more accessible to blind and low-vision visitors. The glasses link to an assistant and to volunteer-guided support, and were shaped by consultation with visually impaired people and sight-loss charities in Italy.
