Experiments suggesting small open-weight AI models may soon self-replicate across poorly defended internet-of-things devices such as smart TV boxes, setting up inference on unfamiliar hardware and spreading slowly like a botnet. Findings cover spread speed, adaptation across copies, and defensive hardening against AI worms.
