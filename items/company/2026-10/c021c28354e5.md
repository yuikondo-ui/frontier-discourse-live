AI agents designing protein binders against scorpion neurotoxins as a route to possible antivenom. Of 211 designs tested in laboratories, 17 met binding criteria, none of the multi-toxin designs bound more than one toxin, and neutralization remains untested. Drug discovery for neglected tropical diseases is affected.
