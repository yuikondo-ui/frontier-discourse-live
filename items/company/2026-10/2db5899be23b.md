Controllable text-to-speech for audiobooks, using large language models to profile characters, attribute dialogue and write stage-direction captions for synthetic voices. Synthetic training data pipelines improve character differentiation and dialogue identification accuracy in English and Spanish tests. Listeners of long-form narration benefit from consistent character voices.
