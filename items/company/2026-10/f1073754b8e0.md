Employment changes inside a frontier AI developer as AI agents spread. Hiring for junior technical roles has slowed while agent hours outnumber employee hours six to one, output per person rises, and human attention becomes the bottleneck, pushing staff toward monitoring agents and valuing judgment over implementation skills.
