A randomized trial with patent attorneys finding that routine AI use raises work quality, but skill gains on unassisted judgment tasks appear only for senior lawyers. Junior lawyers show no average gain and more polarized scores. Concerns arise over whether AI tools prevent juniors from developing expertise.
