AI-assisted blind sweep ultrasound that lets minimally trained health workers with low-cost handheld probes estimate gestational age. A prospective multi-center study finds accuracy comparable to expert sonographers across new clinics and hardware. Pregnant patients in low-resource regions and frontline clinicians are affected.
