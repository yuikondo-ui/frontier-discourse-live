Continual learning for large language models through alternating active use and offline memory consolidation, mitigating catastrophic forgetting and fixed knowledge cutoffs. Experiments show retention of long-context knowledge up to ten million tokens, new factual learning and improved mathematical reasoning compared with fine-tuning baselines.
