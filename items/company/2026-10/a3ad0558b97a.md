Independent third-party review of the test prompts used to evaluate frontier AI model safety, judging diversity, difficulty and methodological soundness rather than model outputs. Reviewers recommended more multilingual and adversarial prompts and fresher test sets, leading to refreshed datasets, difficulty tiers and quarterly reviews. AI safety evaluators are involved.
