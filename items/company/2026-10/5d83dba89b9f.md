Personalized AI student simulators trained on de-identified learner records to evaluate and improve AI tutors before tests with real learners. Simulators match individual students and respond to guidance across chess, second-language writing and mathematics, and give a reward signal that produces higher-rated tutors. Teachers are not replaced.
