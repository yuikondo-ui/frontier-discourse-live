A geospatial foundation model that compresses search trends, mobility and environmental data into location embeddings used in epidemiological forecasting. Five case studies cover vaccination coverage, cardiovascular deaths, dengue, postpartum depression and cholera, finding gains mainly for longer horizons and where official data lag.
