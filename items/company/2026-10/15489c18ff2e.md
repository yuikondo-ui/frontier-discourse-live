Advertising inside chatbots expands to 26 more countries, bringing availability to 89 countries, with over 100,000 advertisers having run campaigns. Ads appear only on free and low-cost plans, are labeled and separated from answers, and conversations stay private from advertisers. Businesses and consumers worldwide are affected.
