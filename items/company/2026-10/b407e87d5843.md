Distillation attacks, in which networks of fraudulent accounts extract the reasoning of frontier AI models to train copycat models with weaker safeguards. New defenses including reasoning controls, classifiers and account bans cut extracted data by 90 to 99 percent. Claims that distilled models raise cyber and national security risks.
