Thousands of AI agents running in parallel to interpret gene-disruption data from millions of mouse brain cells in minutes. The agents link experimental results to scientific literature and propose leads on neurodegeneration, such as a possible connection to cholesterol regulation, which human experts judged novel. Neuroscientists and drug developers are affected.
