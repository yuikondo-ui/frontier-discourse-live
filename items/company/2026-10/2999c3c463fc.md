AI agents that assemble a complete ultraviolet map of the sky by merging space telescope datasets, calibrating them, and predicting unobserved regions with inpainting from other wavelengths. Hidden-patch tests show predictions within about 10 percent of measurements. An astrophysicist guides the agents and corrects errors.
