AI skills training for family caregivers using chatbots. Free in-person workshops in five U.S. cities teach organizing schedules, finding community resources, and using AI responsibly, including protecting personal information and consulting healthcare professionals. Caregivers, patient advocacy groups and people with chronic conditions participate.
