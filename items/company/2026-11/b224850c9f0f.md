Speech recognition built on large language models that cleans noisy analog radio audio and converts firefighting jargon into searchable text. The tool cuts word error rate from 38% to 22%, transcribes hundreds of hours daily, and speeds wildfire alerts. It supports volunteer dispatch monitors at a nonprofit alert service.
