Cost efficiency of coding agents. An open-source plugin learns from past sessions in a repository to avoid rediscovering facts, loading unused tools and repeating failed approaches, cutting costs by 18 to 31 percent across 277 bug-fixing tasks with stable success rates. Software developers are affected.
