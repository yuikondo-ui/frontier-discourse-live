Business adoption of AI across knowledge work, based on interviews with enterprise customers. Findings describe coding agents extending software capabilities to nontechnical roles, few AI-driven layoffs but reduced hiring and contractor use, organizational bottlenecks, and workflow reorganization around improving large language models.
