Backdoor attacks that reprogram dormant neurons in AI models so agents with tool or terminal access run malicious commands when a hidden trigger appears. The backdoors evade standard safety evaluations and need no retraining, making model provenance and weight integrity security priorities. Enterprises deploying open-weight models are affected.
