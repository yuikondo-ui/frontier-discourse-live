Benchmarks for evaluating AI agents on visually complex knowledge work. A new evaluation of 200 expert-designed tasks across ten industries tests whether agents can interpret blueprints, schematics and documents and produce decks, reports and spreadsheets, scored on factual accuracy and visual design. Professionals and AI developers are involved.
