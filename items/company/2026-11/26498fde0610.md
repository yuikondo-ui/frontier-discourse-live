Methods for designing rigorous AI safety evaluations of large language models. Recommendations include assembling domain and methods experts, running evaluations in sandboxes, matching design to causal questions, and measuring appropriate-response rates rather than refusal rates. Relevant to researchers building or relying on model safety testing.
