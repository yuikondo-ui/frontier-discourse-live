Privacy protections for AI-enabled smart glasses that can see their surroundings. Research with wearers, bystanders, and privacy experts shapes design that covers both the wearer and people being filmed, while accessibility features describe surroundings for blind and low-vision people. Bystander consent and recording concerns are central.
