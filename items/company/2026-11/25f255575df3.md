AI systems that passively detect signs of high blood pressure from consumer smartwatch sensors. A new model aims to flag hypertension, which affects over a billion people and often goes undiagnosed, especially among younger adults, complementing office screening. Patients, clinicians and wearable-device users are involved in early cardiovascular risk detection.
