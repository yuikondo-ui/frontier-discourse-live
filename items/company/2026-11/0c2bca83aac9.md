Vulnerability disclosure timelines in the era of AI vulnerability discovery. Because frontier AI models find software flaws much faster, disclosure windows shrink from 90 days to 30 days for high-severity issues and 45 days otherwise, with earlier escalation and shorter patch-to-details delays. Software maintainers and vendors are affected.
