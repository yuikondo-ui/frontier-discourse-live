Online scams across Asia Pacific, including AI-driven phone call impersonation, cause very large financial losses. Defenses include built-in scam detection on phones and messaging, industry coordination on threat intelligence, and funding for community training and AI-powered defense prototypes. Small businesses and consumers are the main groups targeted.
