AI and jobs among workers in Ireland. A survey of 1,000 Irish workers finds 47 percent reconsidering careers because of AI and 63 percent using AI at work, but only 40 percent receiving enough training, with shadow AI creating security and compliance risks. Employers and leaders are involved.
