Watermarking methods for AI-designed biological sequences, giving DNA synthesis providers an automated signal that an order came from a trusted model with safeguards. The method supports biosecurity screening and database integrity, though robustness against tampering remains a challenge. Genome design for viruses that infect bacteria is tested.
