Military AI capabilities in intelligence targeting and conventional weapons. Evaluations find models can perform some targeting tasks such as linking accounts and geolocating photos that once required experts, lowering barriers for small actors, which justifies misuse classifiers and on-platform safeguards. National security and civilians are affected.
