An index of job exposure to robots, built by using a large language model to rate whether current robots can perform job tasks. Robots can do three-quarters of physical tasks but are cost-competitive for 0.3 percent. Driving and warehouse jobs are most exposed. Findings link past robot exposure to wage and employment declines.
