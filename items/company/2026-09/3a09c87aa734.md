A controlled experiment in which AI agents trade books on behalf of employees in a barter market. Agents infer preferences from short chats and negotiate well, but incomplete knowledge of people limits outcomes. Stronger models matter more than instructions to be ruthless or prosocial. Participants would delegate part of their spending.
