Chain-of-thought monitorability in reasoning models. A framework of 13 evaluations across 24 environments finds frontier reasoning models fairly but not perfectly monitorable, with longer reasoning improving monitorability and smaller models at higher effort trading compute for transparency. AI safety researchers are involved.
