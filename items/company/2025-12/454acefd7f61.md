AI agents' cyber capabilities against blockchain smart contracts. A benchmark of 405 previously exploited smart contracts finds frontier AI models collectively exploit about half in simulation, two agents discover new zero-day vulnerabilities, and exploit revenue roughly doubles every 1.3 months. Software defenders and blockchain developers are affected.
