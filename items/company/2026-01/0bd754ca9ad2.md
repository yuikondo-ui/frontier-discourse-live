AI in healthcare for generating radiology reports from medical images. A research prototype combines supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning on clinical-error rewards, and a chest X-ray model trained on 560,000 studies reportedly reaches state-of-the-art accuracy with fewer clinical errors and better generalization across institutions and demographic groups.
