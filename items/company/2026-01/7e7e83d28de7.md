Fine-tuning vision-language models on memory-constrained edge devices. A research method uses only forward passes instead of backpropagation, combining a sharpness-aware evolutionary strategy for initialization with a sparse zeroth-order search and gradient normalization to reduce variance and improve accuracy across 11 tasks.
