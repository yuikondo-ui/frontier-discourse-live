Persona stability in large language models. Interpretability research identifies an Assistant Axis that tracks how assistant-like a model's persona is; drifting away from it enables persona jailbreaks and harmful outputs, while activation capping reduces this. Emotionally sensitive and therapy-like conversations are among the settings affected.
