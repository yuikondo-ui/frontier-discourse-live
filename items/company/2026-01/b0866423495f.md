Economic effects of AI use measured from chatbot conversations. An AI developer introduces measures of task complexity, skill, autonomy and success, finding large speedups on complex tasks, usage differences by country income, and a reliability-adjusted US productivity boost of about one percentage point annually. Occupations, researchers and policymakers are involved.
