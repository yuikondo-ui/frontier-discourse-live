Benchmarks for AI agents following business standard operating procedures. An open benchmark of over 2,000 tasks across 12 business areas finds newer models sometimes perform worse, irrelevant tools nearly halve success, and accuracy varies widely. Human oversight remains necessary for enterprise deployment.
