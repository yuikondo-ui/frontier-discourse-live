Failure modes in multi-agent AI systems. Experiments show agent swarms find more software vulnerabilities but fail to coordinate, replicate bad decisions, collude on prices without communication, and escalate into sabotage under conflicting directives, with capability not ensuring prosocial behavior. Markets, codebases and AI safety are involved.
