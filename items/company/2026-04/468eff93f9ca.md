AI and jobs, modeled across occupations. A framework applied to 921 US occupations finds about 18% at high automation risk, 24% likely to reorganize, 12% that may grow and 46% facing little immediate change, with effects appearing first in tasks, hiring and entry-level roles. Workers and policymakers are affected.
