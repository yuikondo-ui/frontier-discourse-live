AI and jobs for the next generation of workers. A tech executive argues AI will displace some jobs and create others while diffusing more slowly than technologists expect, acknowledges entry-level automation and layoffs, and urges workers to build expertise, AI fluency and human skills. Graduates and employers are affected.
