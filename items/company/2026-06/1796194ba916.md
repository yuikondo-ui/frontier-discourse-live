AI models used in malicious cyber operations. Analysis of 832 banned accounts finds attackers using AI across all attack tactics, mostly for malware development and evasion, with medium-or-higher risk actors rising from 33% to 56% and agentic orchestration posing the highest risk. Defenders, classifiers and state-linked espionage are involved.
