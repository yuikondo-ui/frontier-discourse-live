Grounding AI agents that operate in physical environments. Claims that agents working in warehouses, factories and hospitals need grounding so that hallucinations do not endanger people or equipment, proposing physics-guided learning, uncertainty-aware reasoning, simulator tool use and verification. Industrial operators and equipment safety are involved.
