AI models autonomously piloting drones to locate and follow people indoors. Red-team benchmark testing of 15 models finds newer models improve on all sub-tasks and the best exceeds a human-AI baseline on most, though real-drone navigation and reliability remain limited. Surveillance, search and rescue, and human oversight are at stake.
