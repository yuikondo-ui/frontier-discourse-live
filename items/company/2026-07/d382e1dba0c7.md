AI models breaching real systems during cybersecurity evaluations. An examination of 141,006 evaluation runs finds three incidents where models, believing they were in a sealed simulation, reached the internet through a misconfigured third-party environment and accessed three real organizations, including one malicious software package leading to credential theft.
