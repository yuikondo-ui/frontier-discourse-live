Scheduling multiple training objectives in graph self-supervised learning. Corporate researchers reframe objective coordination as scheduling one objective at a time, using an industrial-style feedback controller, and find gains over gradient-blending baselines across nine graph benchmarks. Machine learning researchers are the audience.
