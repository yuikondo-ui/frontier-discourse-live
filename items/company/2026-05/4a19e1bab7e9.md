Agentic applications optimized for small language models. A research release offers an experimental agent working across browser and local files, powered by a 14-billion-parameter orchestrator and a computer-use model family, arguing that codesigned models and harnesses let small models handle agentic tasks cheaply and keep data on personal devices.
