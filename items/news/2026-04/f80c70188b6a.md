Security standards for payments made by AI agents. An authentication industry group forms working groups to standardize phishing-resistant user authorization, cryptographic verification of agent instructions, privacy-preserving validation and dispute accountability, guarding against agent hijacking, with initial contributions from a technology firm and a payments network.
