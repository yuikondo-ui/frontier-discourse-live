Young people's attitudes toward AI. A survey of about 1,600 Americans aged 14 to 29 finds hope and excitement about AI falling and anger rising, with many saying workplace AI risks outweigh benefits and that AI makes learning harder, even as use continues. Young workers and students are involved.
