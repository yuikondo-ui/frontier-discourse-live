Tech layoffs and AI investment. More than 165,000 tech jobs are cut as companies increase AI spending, while workers describe pressure to use immature AI tools, researchers note AI unreliability, and economists suggest some firms attribute layoffs to AI that stem from overstaffing. Tech workers and software quality are affected.
