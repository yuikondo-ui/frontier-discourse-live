Machine translation errors degrading low-resource languages online. Small-language editions of an online encyclopedia fill with uncorrected machine-translated pages, which then train AI translators, creating a feedback loop of worsening quality. Linguists warn of harm to speakers, farmers seeking information and endangered language communities.
