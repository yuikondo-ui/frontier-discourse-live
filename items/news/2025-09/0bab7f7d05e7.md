Sovereign AI development in South Korea. A national program pledges about $390 million to five domestic organizations building large foundation models, reviewing progress every six months and cutting underperformers, to reduce reliance on foreign AI. Conglomerates, telecom operators and internet firms pursue differing model strategies.
