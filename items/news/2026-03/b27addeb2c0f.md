Claims about chatbots producing medical breakthroughs. Viral claims that a chatbot cured a dog's cancer overstate AI's role: researchers designed a personalized mRNA vaccine, tumors only shrank, and chatbots served mainly for brainstorming. Veterinary medicine, scientists and public understanding of AI are involved.
