AI data centers expanding in the Nordic countries. More than 50 facilities are under construction or planned as developers seek cheap, plentiful renewable power and cool climates over low latency, with GPU cloud providers driving growth and land values rising. Rural municipalities, energy grids and EU emissions targets are involved.
