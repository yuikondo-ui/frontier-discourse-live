AI security / cyberattacks involving agentic AI systems. An eight-step plan for executives treats agents as semi-autonomous users, recommending narrow identities, approved tools, task-bound credentials, untrusted external content, output validation, data tokenization, red-teaming and audit trails, following a documented AI-orchestrated espionage campaign.
