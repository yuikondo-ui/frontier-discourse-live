Workplace culture and enterprise AI adoption. A survey of 500 business leaders finds 83% believe psychological safety improves AI initiative success, while 22% hesitated to lead AI projects for fear of blame, with claims that cultural barriers outweigh technological ones. Employees and corporate leadership are involved.
