AI and jobs, including risks to individual careers. A survey of economists, AI experts and forecasters finds senior occupations such as executives and managers expected to keep growing even under rapid AI progress, while prospects for other white-collar workers remain unclear. Reliable data on AI's economic effects is scarce.
