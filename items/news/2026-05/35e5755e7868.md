Identity and access management for enterprise AI agents. Claims that identity governance, not model capability, keeps most agent pilots from reaching production because human-oriented systems cannot inventory, scope or revoke agent identities at machine speed. Recommendations include human owners, least-privilege access, network telemetry and microsegmentation.
