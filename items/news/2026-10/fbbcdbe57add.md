Personal AI agents acting online as a third kind of web visitor, prompting websites to block them while e-commerce platforms build agent-specific checkouts. Rules on access, negotiation and security remain contested, and the web may be redesigned for agents over people. Consumers, retailers and customer service teams are affected.
