Self-replicating AI worms that spread across low-power internet-connected devices such as smart televisions by splitting a model across them. Researchers say this could become feasible within a year, while security experts dispute its practical significance, noting that basic security hygiene blocks most worms. The debate covers hype and AI security risk.
