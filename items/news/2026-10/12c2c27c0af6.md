Argument that training chatbots to refuse harmful requests is an unreliable foundation for AI safety, since refusal is probabilistic, poorly understood and bypassed by jailbreaks, and cannot be separated from useful capabilities. Concerns include over-refusal, censorship by governments and secret corporate line-drawing.
