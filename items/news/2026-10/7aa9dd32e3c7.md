Workplaces where AI agents act as coworkers handling scheduling, coding, marketing and sales, with startups running far more agents than staff. Savings are weighed against job losses, fewer entry-level openings, errors needing oversight and lost human interaction, while surveys show rising agent adoption without clear productivity gains.
