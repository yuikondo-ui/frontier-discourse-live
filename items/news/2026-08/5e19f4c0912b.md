Security controls for enterprise AI agents. Claims that identity and permission controls are insufficient because agents exploit all granted access at machine scale and prompts can be manipulated. Recommendations include task-scoped permissions, tool-level controls and tiered autonomy requiring human approval for irreversible actions.
