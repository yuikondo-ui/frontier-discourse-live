AI use in the oil and gas industry and its climate effects. A peer-reviewed study estimates that AI productivity gains in extraction, refining and power generation could raise global energy emissions by 1.2 to 4.8 percent, outweighing AI's clean-energy benefits and projected data-center emissions. Technology firms' sustainability claims are questioned.
