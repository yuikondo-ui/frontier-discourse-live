University lecturers using AI to produce teaching materials. A recent doctoral graduate opposes the practice but explains its causes: precarious fixed-term contracts, extensive unpaid preparation, budget cuts and hiring freezes that give early-career academics little incentive to invest preparation time. Students and university staff are affected.
