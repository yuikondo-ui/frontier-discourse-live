Photonic processors that run AI models using light instead of electrons, aiming to cut the energy and cooling costs of AI data centers. They are designed to work alongside GPUs on nonlinear math. A pilot in a cloud provider's facilities is under way, and dozens of engineering teams are building models for the hardware.
