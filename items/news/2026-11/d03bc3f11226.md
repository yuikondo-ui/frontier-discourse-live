A higher education regulator finds generative AI use in essays, dissertations and open-book exams makes coursework assessment close to impossible, urging in-person exams, oral examinations and process-based assessment. Failure to ensure valid assessment may lead to sanctions. Universities, students and the credibility of degrees are affected.
