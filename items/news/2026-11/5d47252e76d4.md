Mandatory disclosure of AI use applies to newly uploaded video game mods, with public explanations for AI-assisted work, more prominent labels and controls to hide AI-generated content. The platform declines an outright ban, will not use mods to train AI, and sets a 60-day grace period. Modding communities are affected.
