Generative AI features that let phone owners create custom home-screen widgets from text prompts. Testing finds the tool fun for simple, templated ideas but limited for practical tasks such as real-time transit information, since many widgets are shortcuts to a chatbot. The beta feature is rolling out to a small set of phones.
