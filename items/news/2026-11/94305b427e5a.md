Shipments of AI chips and servers to Southeast Asian countries are paused while a chipmaker checks whether regional partners meet export requirements. Chinese firms had accessed restricted AI chips remotely through data centers there, a loophole recently closed, and such access is estimated to supply about a quarter of China's AI computing power.
