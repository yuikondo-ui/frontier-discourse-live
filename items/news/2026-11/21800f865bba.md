AI use in US retail is expanding across scheduling, discipline, safety alerts and surveillance of workers and shoppers. Workers describe inaccurate automated alerts that interrupt customer service, while labor advocates call for guardrails, regulation and collective bargaining protections. Retailers say the tools support employees, and industry surveys show growing adoption and investment.
