A state AI safety law taking effect requires large frontier AI developers above $500 million revenue to register, publish safety frameworks, disclose critical safety incidents within 72 hours and issue transparency reports, with penalties up to $3 million. A new oversight office and research center evaluate advanced AI systems. Frontier AI labs are affected.
