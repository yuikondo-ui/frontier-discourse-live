Two vulnerabilities in an AI-driven debt collection platform exposed millions of borrower accounts and hundreds of thousands of call recordings from its autonomous call center. Independent research found the flaws, since patched, which exposed sensitive financial information and raise concerns for agentic systems used by large fintech firms.
