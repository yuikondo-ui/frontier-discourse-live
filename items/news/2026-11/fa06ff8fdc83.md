An AI assistant in a recipe app that adapts recipes for ingredient swaps, scaling, equipment substitutions and unit conversion, trained partly on reader comments. Tests find it practical and cautious about absurd requests, but it occasionally errs on quantities and confuses which recipe version it uses. Home cooks are the main users.
