Conversational chatbots in music streaming services. A streaming platform tests a chatbot feature that lets subscribers request and explore music, audiobooks and podcasts by text or voice, using personal listening history and follow-up instructions, after a rival launched a similar feature. Subscribers in the US, Ireland and Sweden are involved.
