Human writing styles in response to AI-generated text. Writers develop an anti-AI style avoiding recognizable AI tells such as em dashes and lists of three, favoring idiosyncrasy, anecdotes and deliberate typos, while editors receive mostly AI-written pitches and publishing faces AI fabrication controversies. Novelists, journalists and editors are involved.
