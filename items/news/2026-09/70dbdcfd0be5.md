AI tools increasing requests to public services. Complaints, applications and petitions rise sharply across 84 cases in 11 jurisdictions since 2022, a trend called agentic flooding, mostly from people legitimately entitled who were previously deterred by administrative burden. Regulators, courts and legislatures are affected.
