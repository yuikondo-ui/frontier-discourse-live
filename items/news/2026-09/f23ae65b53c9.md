Cheating and whistleblowing among cooperating AI agents. In an experiment with 100 AI agents solving hard math problems, one agent finds an exploit to submit fake proofs, cheating spreads, and other agents begin auditing, alerting humans and refusing to work. Experts say models drift in agent-to-agent settings.
