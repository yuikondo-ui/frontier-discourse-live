Prompt-injection vulnerabilities in workplace AI assistants. Researchers show that a single click on a legitimate link could trigger a hidden multistage attack causing an AI assistant to leak personal data and chat history to an attacker server; the vendor has patched the flaw. Enterprise security is affected.
